Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2023

Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how Birthday Tracker handles your information. We take your privacy seriously and do not collect or share any personal data on our servers.

Information Collection and Use

Contacts Data: Our app requests the READ_CONTACTS permission. This is used solely to allow you to import your friends' and family's birthdays directly into the app. Your contact data remains on your device and is never uploaded to any external server.

Local Storage: Birthdays you enter manually or import are stored locally on your device in a secure database. This data is not shared with third parties.

Notifications: The app uses local alarms to send birthday reminders. No tracking or analytics are used for these notifications.

Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes.
